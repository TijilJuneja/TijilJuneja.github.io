Kingdoms: Logic Grid — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026 (version 1.0.1)

Kingdoms: Logic Grid ("the app") is a single-player puzzle game. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: we (the developer) don't collect any personal data and have no servers. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to show them. Buying the one-time unlock removes all ads.

What we collect

Nothing ourselves. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no servers of its own. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files. The only third-party data collection is by Google for ads (see "Ads" below) and by Google Play for purchases.

What stays on your device

The app stores the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • your puzzle progress (floors climbed, best times, daily streak);
  • unfinished games, so you can continue where you left off;
  • your settings (sound, haptics, theme, assists);
  • whether you have bought the one-time unlock, and how many free hints you used today;
  • a small ad counter (how many puzzles you solved since the last ad, and when it was shown), so ads stay infrequent.

This data never leaves your device through the app. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, it is deleted. Android's own device backup may include app data if you have turned backup on in your system settings; that is handled by Google under your Google account settings, not by us.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app shows a limited number of ads using Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK), provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited:

  • an occasional full-screen ad between puzzles (never during a puzzle, never in the first 10 floors, at most once every 3 solved puzzles and a few minutes apart);
  • an optional short video you can choose to watch for an extra hint. It only plays when you tap "Watch".

There are no banner ads. To choose and show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID) and the app set ID;
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (country/city level);
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, app version and screen size;
  • ad interactions, such as ads shown, taps and video views, and diagnostic information about the SDK's performance.

All of this data is encrypted in transit (TLS). It is collected by Google, not by us; we never receive it in a form that identifies you. Google's use is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices:

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland, and other regions where required): when you open the free version, Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) asks whether you agree to personalised ads. If you don't consent, Google shows only non-personalised or limited ads. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings › Ad privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Android: Settings › Privacy › Ads; iOS: the app never asks to track you, so ads on iOS are not personalised using tracking).
  • Google ad settings: adssettings.google.com lets you control personalised ads from Google.
  • Remove ads completely: buying the one-time unlock removes all ads. Once the unlock is on your device the app stops loading ads and does not start the ads SDK.

Because the data is held by Google, it can't be deleted from inside the app; use Google's controls above (or contact Google) for requests about ad data.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The app offers an optional one-time purchase ("Unlock everything"). It unlocks every floor, Endless mode and unlimited hints, and removes all ads. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or receive your name, email address, card or other payment details. Google Play tells the app only whether the unlock has been purchased for your Google account, so that it can be restored on your devices. Google's handling of this information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

Network access

The app has no servers. Network communication comes only from Google Play's billing service (when you open the unlock screen, buy, or restore a purchase) and, for free players, from the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google's consent service. The game itself works fully offline; if there is no connection, no ads are shown.

Children

The app is intended for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. It has no chat or social features. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child under 13 is using the free version with ads, buying the unlock removes ads, or contact us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above before the version with the change is released.
